# Business context (for development & landing page)

## Product
Software for fitness businesses: **shared platform core** + **per-client extensions**.

Addresses universal operational pains (sessions, coaches, packages, scheduling, cancellation policy) through a core platform, extended with workflows and rules tailored per client.

## Target customers (landing & outreach focus)
**Primary (page leads with these):**
- Growing / small fitness companies that **already know they need software** (still on WhatsApp, sheets, calendar)
- Companies on **generic software** that lack personalization / completion for their workflows

**Deprioritized for now:**
- Companies that do not yet know they need software (education-heavy)
- Companies on personalized software seeking a full replacement (higher proof bar)

**Vertical context:** personal training & home service, coaching studios, rehab-oriented fitness — but buyer stage matters more than vertical on the page.

## Key benefit
Operational visibility and automation so operators scale coaching without spreadsheet/WhatsApp chaos.

## Pain points we lead with (from market research)
- Scale without operational chaos
- Business-wide control and visibility
- Session & package tracking (no manual counting)
- Coach scheduling: availability, reschedules, utilization
- Package expiration automation
- **Late cancellation policy enforcement** (critical for home-service PT)

## Positioning (no clients / no company yet)
- Discovery-first outreach — conversation before pitch
- No fake testimonials or "trusted by" claims
- Honest: shared core + tailored extensions, built with operator input
- Landing page goal: attract operators who recognize these pains and want to talk

## Not in scope
- Consumer workout / fitness apps
- One identical product sold unchanged to every client
- Hard sell before understanding client workflow

## Strategic Q&A
1. **Key benefit:** Control + automation for session-based fitness operations
2. **Customers:** PT company owners, studio operators, rehab-oriented fitness businesses
3. **What they want:** See everything, automate policies, stop manual tracking, grow without admin burden
4. **Where to find them:** Industry networks, LinkedIn, fitness business communities, warm intros, targeted outreach to companies outgrowing manual tools
5. **How to talk today:** Landing page → email/discovery call → learn workflow → shape offer
